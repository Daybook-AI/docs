## 2. USER — Who experiences it?

The problem is experienced by **people who actively want to understand themselves, reflect on their experiences, and improve over time**, but struggle to turn their thoughts into useful, lasting insights.

### Primary users

- **Students**
  - Want to understand their learning habits and progress.
  - Struggle to identify what is holding them back.
  - Want to reflect on their goals, mistakes, and growth.

- **Entrepreneurs & founders**
  - Make many decisions and face constant challenges.
  - Want to understand their decision-making patterns, productivity, and recurring problems.
  - Need a way to reflect without spending a lot of time documenting everything.

- **Creative people**
  - Writers, designers, developers, artists, content creators, etc.
  - Generate many ideas and thoughts that are easily forgotten.
  - Want to understand what helps or blocks their creativity.

- **Self-improvement-focused people**
  - Actively work on habits, productivity, mindset, or personal goals.
  - Want evidence of whether they are actually improving.
  - Want to discover recurring behaviors and patterns.

### Common characteristics

These users generally:

- Have **many thoughts, experiences, and ideas** they want to preserve.
- Care about **personal growth and self-reflection**.
- Want to understand **why they behave or perform in certain ways**.
- Don't want to spend a lot of time maintaining complicated journals.
- Would value being able to **ask questions about their own history**.
- Want their past experiences to become **useful knowledge rather than forgotten memories**.

### The important part

Your customer isn't simply:

> **"Someone who likes journaling."**

It's more specifically:

> **"Someone who wants to understand themselves and improve, but doesn't have an easy way to turn their everyday experiences and reflections into useful insights."**

That distinction is important because it means your product can serve people who **don't currently journal at all**, as long as you make capturing their thoughts extremely easy.
