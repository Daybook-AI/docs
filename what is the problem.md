## 1. PROBLEM — What problem exists?

- **Our thoughts, experiences, and lessons are easily lost.**  
  We think about many things every day, but most of those thoughts disappear or become difficult to recall later.

- **People have no structured personal memory of their own journey.**  
  A normal diary stores what happened, but it doesn't help us understand how our thoughts, behavior, and decisions have changed over time.

- **It is difficult to identify recurring problems.**  
  We may repeatedly procrastinate, lose motivation, make the same mistakes, or struggle with the same situations without realizing the pattern.

- **It is difficult to measure personal improvement.**  
  We may feel that we are improving or getting worse, but we don't have enough historical information to compare our current self with our past self.

- **Important insights are buried inside unstructured writing.**  
  Even when someone journals, useful information can be scattered across hundreds of entries and become difficult to find.

- **Reflection is usually disconnected from action.**  
  We may recognize a problem today, but later forget what we learned, what we planned to change, or whether we actually improved.

- **There is no easy way to ask questions about our own history.**  
  Questions such as:
  - *What has been holding me back?*
  - *What problems keep repeating?*
  - *What have I improved at?*
  - *What goals have I abandoned?*
  - *What makes me productive or unproductive?*
  - *What patterns have appeared in my life?*

  are difficult to answer from ordinary diary entries.

### Core problem

> **People generate a huge amount of personal experience, thoughts, and insights, but they lack a system that preserves this information and turns it into meaningful, searchable self-knowledge.**

That is the problem your application is trying to solve.

And this gives you a very important distinction:

**Journaling is the input.  
Personal memory is the data.  
Analysis is the intelligence.  
Self-understanding and improvement are the outcome.**
